How to Write a Great Job Application Email

  
  1. 1Open with the specific role and where you found it. Hiring managers scan dozens of applications — make yours instantly clear.
  2. 2Lead with your most relevant achievement, not your education or years of experience. Results > credentials.
  3. 3Show you know the company. Reference their product, mission, or a recent news item to prove this isn't a mass application.
  4. 4Close with enthusiasm and a clear next step. 'I'd love to discuss how my experience in X can help with Y' is stronger than 'I look forward to hearing from you.'

Tips for Better Job Application Emails

  
Hiring managers spend an average of 7.4 seconds on initial resume/application scans. Your opening sentence must hook them. Quantified achievements ('grew revenue by 40%') are 3x more memorable than vague claims ('experienced in sales').

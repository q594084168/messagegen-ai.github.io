How to Write a Great Email Writing Email

  
  1. 1Start with your goal in mind. Ask yourself: what do I want the recipient to do after reading this email? That becomes your subject line and opening.
  2. 2Use natural language — write like you speak in a professional meeting. Stiff, overly formal language creates distance.
  3. 3Break long paragraphs into short ones. Each paragraph should make exactly one point. White space is your friend.
  4. 4Proofread aloud before sending. If you stumble reading it, rewrite that sentence.

How to Write a Great Professional Email

  
  1. 1Match the formality level to your audience and relationship. Internal emails can be more casual than client-facing ones.
  2. 2Use a clear, descriptive subject line. 'Q3 Marketing Budget Approval Needed' beats 'Quick question.'
  3. 3Front-load the key ask or information. Don't bury the important part in paragraph three.
  4. 4End with clear next steps and a timeline. 'Please review by Friday' creates accountability.

Tips for Better Professional Emails

  
Professional emails with numbered or bulleted key points get 30% more engagement than wall-of-text paragraphs. Always ask yourself: if the recipient only reads the first sentence, will they get the gist?

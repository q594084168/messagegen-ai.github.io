25 Email Subject Line Formulas That Boost Open Rates

Published 2026-06-01 · MessageGen-AI Blog

Your subject line is the most important 40 characters you'll write today. It determines whether your email gets opened or ignored. Here are 25 formulas that work, organized by psychological trigger.

Curiosity Gap Formulas

"The one thing most [professionals] get wrong about [topic]" — creates an information gap the recipient needs to close. "I tried [something] for 30 days. Here's what happened." — narrative curiosity. "Quick question about [their company/role]" — personal curiosity.

Urgency + Scarcity Formulas

"Closing Friday: [opportunity]" — deadline-driven. "Only 3 spots left for [event]" — scarcity. "Before you [do something], read this" — preemptive urgency. Use sparingly — overuse triggers spam filters.

Personalization Formulas

"[Name], your thoughts on [topic]?" — name + open question. "Saw your post about [topic] — had an idea" — reference their content. "Following up on our conversation at [event]" — shared context.

Value-First Formulas

"How to [achieve desired outcome] in [timeframe]" — actionable promise. "[Number] ways to [improve something]" — listicle format. "I found a way to [solve their problem]" — direct value. These consistently outperform clever/creative subject lines in B2B contexts.

How to Write a Great Sales Email

  
  1. 1Focus on the prospect's pain points, not your product features. They care about solving their problem, not your tech stack.
  2. 2Include social proof — a specific result from a similar company, a recognizable client name, or a quantified outcome.
  3. 3Make the CTA crystal clear and low-friction. 'Reply with a time that works' beats 'Let me know if you're interested.'
  4. 4Keep the email scannable. Use short paragraphs, bullet points for key benefits, and bold the most important number or claim.

Tips for Better Sales Emails

  
The best sales emails don't feel like sales emails. They feel like a smart colleague sharing a relevant insight. Lead with the recipient's world, not yours. Your product should appear as the bridge between their problem and their goal.

The Ultimate Professional Email Templates Library: 20 Free Templates

Published 2026-06-15 · MessageGen-AI Blog

Bookmark this page. Here are 20 professional email templates you can adapt and use immediately — no sign-up, no paywall, just copy, paste, and personalize.

Meeting & Scheduling (Templates 1-5)

Meeting Request: "Hi [Name], I'd like to schedule [duration] to discuss [topic]. I believe this could help with [their goal/pain point]. Would [Option A] or [Option B] work? I'll send a brief agenda beforehand." Meeting Confirmation: "Confirming our call on [date] at [time]. Agenda: [3 bullet points]. Call link: [link]." Reschedule Request: "Apologies — I need to reschedule our [date] call due to [brief reason]. Would [new date/time] work instead?"

Client & Project (Templates 6-10)

Project Update: "Quick update on [project]: [status]. Key milestone: [achievement]. Next step: [action] by [date]." Proposal Submission: "As discussed, here's our proposal for [project]. Key points: [3 bullets]. I'm happy to walk through it on a call — let me know if [date/time] works."

Internal & Career (Templates 11-20)

Resignation letter, thank-you email, introduction email, sick day notification, out-of-office message, feedback request, performance review summary, congratulations email, farewell email, and promotion announcement — all included with ready-to-use templates.

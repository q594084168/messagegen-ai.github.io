How to Write a Great Email Writing Email

  
  1. 1Be specific in your description. Instead of 'write a professional email,' say 'email to my manager asking to work from home on Fridays during summer.'
  2. 2Include context: who is the recipient, what's your relationship, what outcome do you want?
  3. 3Mention the desired tone — formal, friendly, persuasive, apologetic, urgent, etc.
  4. 4Add any constraints: word count limit, must include certain points, must avoid certain topics.

Tips for Better Email Writing Emails

  
The quality of the generated email directly depends on the quality of your description. Include WHO it's for, WHAT you want to communicate, WHY it matters, and HOW you want it to sound. The more context you provide, the better the result.

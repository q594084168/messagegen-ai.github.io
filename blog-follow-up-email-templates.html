7 Follow-Up Email Templates That Get Responses (Without Being Pushy)

Published 2026-05-15 · MessageGen-AI Blog

The difference between a deal closed and a deal lost is often one follow-up email. But writing them is awkward — you don't want to sound desperate or pushy. Here are 7 templates for every follow-up situation.

1. Sales Follow-Up (3 Days After Pitch)

"Hi [Name], I know you're processing a lot of info. One thing I wanted to flag: [specific benefit] seems particularly relevant to [their company] because [reason]. Would a 15-minute call next Tuesday or Thursday work to discuss?"

2. Job Application Follow-Up (1 Week After Applying)

"Hi [Hiring Manager], I applied for the [Role] position last week and wanted to follow up. I've been following [Company]'s recent work on [project/initiative] and would love to discuss how my experience in [relevant skill] could contribute. I've attached my resume for reference."

3. Networking Follow-Up (Next Day)

"Hi [Name], great meeting you at [event] yesterday. I really enjoyed our conversation about [topic]. I'd love to stay in touch — let me know if you're ever open to a quick virtual coffee. In the meantime, here's that article I mentioned: [link]."

4-7. Client Check-In, Post-Meeting, Invoice Follow-Up, Re-engagement

Client Check-In: "Hi [Client], it's been a month since we launched [project]. How's everything running? Any issues or feedback? I'd love to hear what's working and what could be better." Invoice Follow-Up: "Hi [Client], just a friendly reminder that invoice #1234 for $2,500 is now 15 days past due. Let me know if there are any questions or if you need us to resend it." Post-Meeting: "Thanks for the productive call today. Here's a quick summary of next steps: [list]. I'll send the detailed proposal by [date]."

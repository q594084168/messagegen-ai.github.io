How to Write a Great Reply Email

  
  1. 1Acknowledge their message first. Even if you disagree, show you've read and understood their point before responding.
  2. 2Address each concern or question individually. Don't skip anything — it makes people feel unheard.
  3. 3Be specific about next steps. Vague replies like 'I'll look into it' create frustration. Give timelines.
  4. 4Match their communication style. If they wrote a formal email, reply formally. If casual, keep it conversational.

Tips for Better Reply Emails

  
When replying to difficult messages, wait at least 30 minutes before responding. Emotional emails deserve a drafted-but-not-sent response, then a revision the next morning. The goal is to de-escalate while still being direct.

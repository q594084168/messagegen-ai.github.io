How to Write a Great Meeting Request Email

  
  1. 1State the purpose and expected duration upfront. '15 minutes to discuss X' is much more appealing than 'I'd like to meet.'
  2. 2Explain what's in it for them. Why should they give you their time? Frame the meeting around their benefit.
  3. 3Offer specific time slots instead of asking 'when works?' Reducing back-and-forth increases booking rates.
  4. 4Include a brief agenda so they know exactly what to expect and can prepare if needed.

Tips for Better Meeting Request Emails

  
Meeting requests with specific time slots get 2x more responses than open-ended asks. Always include the meeting length — '15 minutes' is far less intimidating than 'a meeting.' If you can, attach a brief agenda document.

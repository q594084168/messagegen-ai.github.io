How to Write a Great Cold Outreach Email

  
  1. 1Research your recipient — mention something specific about their work, company, or recent achievement to show you're not mass-blasting.
  2. 2Lead with value, not your pitch. Open with something relevant to them before introducing yourself.
  3. 3Keep it under 150 words. Busy people scan emails in seconds — every sentence must earn its place.
  4. 4End with a clear, low-commitment call-to-action like 'Would a 15-minute call work?' instead of 'Let me know when you're free.'

Tips for Better Cold Outreach Emails

  
Cold emails with personalized subject lines get 50% higher open rates. Always reference something specific about the recipient — their LinkedIn post, a company announcement, or a mutual connection. Avoid spam trigger words like 'free,' 'guaranteed,' or 'act now.'

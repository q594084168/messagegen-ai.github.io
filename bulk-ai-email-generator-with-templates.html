How to Write a Great Email Writing Email

  
  1. 1Hook them in the subject line. A great subject line is specific, creates curiosity, and promises value — not 'Update' but 'Q3 numbers: 23% growth + 2 risks to watch'.
  2. 2Put the action item in the first sentence. Recipients scan, they don't read. If there's something they need to do, say it immediately.
  3. 3Use the 'So What' test on every sentence. If a sentence doesn't add information the recipient needs, delete it.
  4. 4Schedule send for optimal timing. Tuesday-Thursday between 8-10am and 2-4pm have the highest open rates.

Tips for Better Email Writing Emails

  
Emails with 3 or fewer asks get 2x more responses than emails with 5+ asks. If you need multiple things, send separate emails or number them clearly. Decision fatigue is real — make it easy for people to say yes.
